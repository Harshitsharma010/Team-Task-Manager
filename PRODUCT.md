# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nexus serves small project teams that need one dependable place to review delivery health, coordinate ownership, and move assigned work through a shared workflow. It also needs to communicate production-minded frontend and full-stack craft clearly to recruiters evaluating the portfolio project.

## Product Purpose

Nexus brings projects, role-aware membership, assigned tasks, deadlines, comments, and workspace analytics into a protected team workspace. The product succeeds when a user can quickly understand what needs attention, see who owns the work, and take the next useful action without interpreting decorative dashboard noise.

## Brand Personality

Focused, credible, and efficient. The interface should feel calm under a busy workload, precise enough for professional teams, and polished enough to support the project's portfolio role.

## Anti-references

Avoid toy-like personal todo apps, oversaturated Kanban boards, oversized marketing copy inside authenticated screens, generic SaaS metric-card galleries, and decorative technical language that competes with the user's work.

## Design Principles

- Lead with attention: surface overdue, due-soon, review, and ownership signals before general totals.
- Make the next action obvious: every dashboard region should support a decision or a direct workflow.
- Earn density: keep useful team context visible while using hierarchy and spacing to prevent clutter.
- Use one interaction language: navigation, controls, badges, focus states, and feedback should stay consistent across the app.
- Show product maturity through behavior: responsive structure, useful empty states, clear loading states, and accessible interactions matter more than decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve clear keyboard focus, semantic labels, meaningful status text that does not rely on color alone, comfortable touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives for every animated state.
